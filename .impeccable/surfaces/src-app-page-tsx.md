# Portfolio homepage

Scope: replace the visual world and copy on the public one-page portfolio. Mode: Experience.

Audience: potential clients, collaborators, recruiters, and startup hiring teams. They need to assess fit quickly and have a clear way to contact John. Proof is limited to the supplied profile, 3+ years building web products, and actual skills. Projects and work history are empty; keep those states honest. Keep content facts in portfolio.json.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the profile and technical range read as one connected practice through an architectural cutaway. Avoid a generic hero followed by detached technology cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt, coral-red, acid-yellow, chalk, and graphite planes with precise section marks and strong editorial type. Use the layered section drawing as the signature, with quiet space around labels and portrait.

STORY: Hiring teams see who John is, what he builds, and the tools he uses within seconds; concise natural copy leads into the real skills and honest empty work history. The primary action opens Contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Use `.impeccable/mocks/cutaway-variant-four.png` as the approved layout. A wide profile field occupies the left, with name and role large, short positioning and description below, then years and one clear contact action. John’s portrait bridges the profile field and three stacked capability planes on the right. Start Selected projects at the lower edge. Keep `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.png` as the visual reference for the connected architectural cutaway, portrait placement, and drafting detail. Rewrite copy in direct, brief, conversational English, without inflated claims.

CAPABILITY FLOW: Technology labels come from the structured skills content. Desktop wall groups keep a fixed number of marks on each panel; when a group exceeds that capacity, its visible window advances one technology at a time along the wall and loops indefinitely. Pause rotation while hovered, keyboard-focused, offscreen, or while the document is hidden. Under reduced motion, keep the wall stationary and continue overflow below the artwork. Mobile lists remain static and show every technology in normal page flow. Do not clip labels or add nested scrolling.

FORM: The Cutaway Folio, assigned direction (position 1 in the direction shortlist), seed key 14cc5560. The user approved Variant 4 as the base and the original decision comp as reference.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
